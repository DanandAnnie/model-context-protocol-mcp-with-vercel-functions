Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

Staging Inventory Manager ("the App") helps home staging professionals manage property inventories, track items, and coordinate with their team. Your privacy matters to us.

Data We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and name when you sign up via Supabase Auth.
  • Inventory data: Property details, item records, room measurements, payment and expense records that you enter.
  • Calendar data: When you connect Google Calendar, we access your calendar events to display scheduling information. We do not store your Google data on our servers.
  • Device information: Browser type and operating system for compatibility purposes.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the inventory management service
  • To sync data across your devices in real-time
  • To enable team collaboration features
  • To display your Google Calendar events within the app (when connected)

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL database hosted on AWS). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We also cache data locally on your device using IndexedDB for offline access.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Database, authentication, and real-time sync (Privacy Policy)
  • Vercel: Application hosting (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Calendar API: Calendar integration, only when you explicitly connect your account (Privacy Policy)

Google Calendar Data

When you connect Google Calendar, we request read/write access to your calendar events. Your Google access tokens are stored only in your browser's local storage — never on our servers. You can disconnect at any time by revoking access in your Google Account settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Data is only shared within your team as configured by you.

Your Rights

  • Access, update, or delete your account and data at any time
  • Export your inventory data
  • Disconnect third-party integrations (Google Calendar)
  • Request complete data deletion by contacting us

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us through the app or at the email provided in your account settings.
